Things to try

    Gluebox

    Choose which faces of a cube are glued together, and how. Then fly around inside the space you made. Light and you both go straight through a glued face and carry on out of its partner.

    Start from a known space

    Your cube (drag to turn it)

    hyperbolicflatspherical

    Faces of the same colour are glued together. The F on one face lands on the F of the same colour, so you can read off the twist and any mirror flip. Edges of the same colour end up as one edge (dark end → light end shows the direction); dashed edges are where the angles don't add up to 360°. Curving space changes the cube's corner angles, which can make a space smooth that a flat cube can't.

    Faces

    FaceGlued toTwistFlip

    Twist turns the face by quarter turns (counter-clockwise seen from outside) before gluing. Flip mirrors it left-to-right. Gluing a face to itself folds it.
