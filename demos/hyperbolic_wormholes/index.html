Things to try

  1. The rails. The coloured walls all leave the long cobblestone wall at right angles, 3 blocks apart. In flat space they'd stay parallel. Here every wall is straight and they still fan apart, exponentially. Press Above the rails.
  2. Five squares at every corner. The stone roads outline identical square tiles with 72° corners, so five meet at every crossing.
  3. The mouth ahead on the left bends light like a black hole of the same size, but where its shadow would be you see the house door, through the other mouth. Press G then F to fly through.
  4. Turn around. The mouth by the blue pylon shows the red pylon. That pylon is about 50 blocks away down the rails, where hyperbolic space has room for hundreds of blocks between neighbouring rails. The wormhole makes it a few seconds' flight.
  5. The mouth on the far left comes out high above the rails, looking down. From up there the infinite ground shrinks to a disk below you.
  6. Go in off-centre or from the side and you come out tilted: the throat is a sphere, not a doorway. The view levels out after a moment.
  7. Place your own with B. Until it has an exit, an entrance leads into itself: you come out of its far side.
  8. Slide R to 14 for gentler curvature, or 5 for much stronger.
Physics choices: light follows straight lines (geodesics) of H³. Near each mouth it bends by the flat wormhole demo's lensing law, written with hyperbolic space's own version of 1/distance (the potential of a point mass in H³). So a mouth looks like the flat one up close, and its pull fades exponentially beyond R. Each mouth bends light only within 2R, and several mouths' bending is added up, which is an approximation. You fly along light rays, so the crosshair shows where you will go.
