Things to try

  1. The laser goes perfectly straight (along a geodesic), so from behind it always looks straight. Aim level and it never comes down: the ground falls away from a straight line faster than the line can drop. You have to aim down by more than a few degrees to hit the ground at all (the stats show where it lands). Press Laser fan (V) to see a whole range of angles from the side.
  2. The rails. The coloured walls all start at right angles to the same straight cobblestone wall, 3 blocks apart. In flat space they'd be parallel forever. Here every wall is perfectly straight and they still fan apart, exponentially. Press Above the rails. Leave emitters on two neighbouring rails, pointing along them: the beams spread apart too.
  3. Square tiles, but five at a corner. The stone roads outline identical square tiles. Each one has 72° corners, so five meet at every crossing instead of four.
  4. Room grows exponentially. Watch the circumference readout as you walk away from the start. Distant things shrink exponentially, and there's far more "world" at each distance than in flat space.
  5. Fly way up (E). The ground is an infinite plane, yet from high up it shrinks to a small disk below you, and the horizon sinks far below eye level.
  6. Slide R to 12 for a gentler curvature.
Laser choices: a beam that hits nothing fades out slowly (it never comes back in hyperbolic space) and is drawn out to 10R, by which point it has shrunk to a point on the horizon.
