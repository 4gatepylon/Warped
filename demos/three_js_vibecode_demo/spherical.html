Things to try

  1. The triangle. You start on top of the red tower (Q to come down). The cobblestone walls are perfectly straight (they're geodesics), and they meet here at 90°. Walk to the blue or yellow tower: those corners are 90° too. One triangle, three right angles.
  2. Look up. The ground wraps around over your head. The upside-down trees up there are on the far side of the world.
  3. Walk away from the house (or watch the far towers). In flat space things shrink with distance. Here they shrink until πR/2 away, then grow again.
  4. Auto-walk (F) in a straight line: after 2πR blocks you're back where you started.
  5. Ground off, then look behind you. Light circles the whole universe, so you see the back of every object in the opposite direction.
  6. Slide R up to 64 and the same house looks almost normal.
