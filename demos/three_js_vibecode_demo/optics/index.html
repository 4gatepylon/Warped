Optics lab (flat space)

Each demo adds one optical element to the previous one.

  1. Mirrors: a zigzag, a periscope, two facing mirrors and a corner reflector.
  2. Beam splitters: a 1-into-4 cascade and a Mach–Zehnder layout.
  3. Diffraction: gratings with red, green, blue or white light.
  4. Heat haze: hot air above heaters bends a beam and spreads it into a cone.
  5. Refraction: glass blocks, a prism that splits white light, and a glass bar that traps a beam.
