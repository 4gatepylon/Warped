Things to try

  1. The rails. The coloured walls all start at right angles to the same straight cobblestone wall, 3 blocks apart. In flat space they'd be parallel forever. Here every wall is perfectly straight and they still fan apart, exponentially. Press Above the rails.
  2. Square tiles, but five at a corner. The stone roads outline identical square tiles. Each one has 72° corners, so five meet at every crossing instead of four.
  3. Room grows exponentially. Watch the circumference readout as you walk away from the start. Distant things shrink exponentially, and there's far more "world" at each distance than in flat space.
  4. Fly way up (E). The ground is an infinite plane, yet from high up it shrinks to a small disk below you, and the horizon sinks far below eye level.
  5. Slide R to 12 for a gentler curvature.
