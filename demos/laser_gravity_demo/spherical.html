Things to try

  1. Gravity pulls you and the laser toward the ground under your feet (not the ground you see overhead), along the shortest way down, at Earth strength (1 block = 1 m). Real light falls far too little to see, so the laser falls as if fired at 20 blocks/s. The slider changes that, down to off.
  2. Gravity and the geometry pull the same way here. Even with no gravity, a level shot lands exactly πR/2 blocks away from any height. Gravity makes it land sooner. Press Laser fan (V) on the spawn tower to see a range of angles from the side.
  3. Turn the ground off (it only hides it from the laser and from view; you still stand on it). Now a beam falls through where the ground was, gets pulled back from the other side, and keeps swinging back and forth across it, never repeating. It would go on forever, so it fades slowly and is cut off once it is dim. With the gravity slider at 0 it instead goes all the way around the universe and comes back to your gun from behind after exactly 2πR, drawn once around.
  4. The triangle. You start on top of the red tower (walk off the edge to come down). The cobblestone walls are perfectly straight (they're geodesics), and they meet here at 90°. Walk to the blue or yellow tower: those corners are 90° too. One triangle, three right angles.
  5. Look up. The ground wraps around over your head. The upside-down trees up there are on the far side of the world.
  6. Walk away from the house (or watch the far towers). In flat space things shrink with distance. Here they shrink until πR/2 away, then grow again.
  7. Auto-walk (F) in a straight line: after 2πR blocks you're back where you started.
  8. Ground off, then look behind you. Light circles the whole universe, so you see the back of every object in the opposite direction.
  9. Slide R up to 64 and the same house looks almost normal.
