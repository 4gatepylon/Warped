Things to try

  1. Gravity pulls you and the laser toward the ground plane under you, along the shortest way down, at Earth strength (1 block = 1 m). Real light falls far too little to see, so the laser falls as if fired at 20 blocks/s. The slider changes that.
  2. Gravity against the geometry. Without gravity a level beam never comes down here: the ground curves away from a straight line. Gravity pulls it back, but the curving-away gets stronger the higher you are. Below a certain height gravity wins and level shots land; above it they still sail off forever (the note under the slider gives that height). Jetpack up (Space) and watch the live beam change from landing to escaping. Press Laser fan (V) to see a range of angles from the side.
  3. The rails. The coloured walls all start at right angles to the same straight cobblestone wall, 3 blocks apart. In flat space they'd be parallel forever. Here every wall is perfectly straight and they still fan apart, exponentially. Press Above the rails. Leave emitters on two neighbouring rails, pointing along them: the beams spread apart too.
  4. Square tiles, but five at a corner. The stone roads outline identical square tiles. Each one has 72° corners, so five meet at every crossing instead of four.
  5. Room grows exponentially. Watch the circumference readout as you walk away from the start. Distant things shrink exponentially, and there's far more "world" at each distance than in flat space.
  6. Fly way up (switches fly mode on). The ground is an infinite plane, yet from high up it shrinks to a small disk below you, and the horizon sinks far below eye level.
  7. Slide R to 12 for a gentler curvature.
Choices: gravity has the same strength everywhere and always points at the nearest point of the ground plane (from below the plane, back up). It turns the laser but never slows it. A beam that hits nothing fades out slowly and is drawn out to 10R, by which point it has shrunk to a point on the horizon. You can still walk where the tiled ground ends: the plane holds you up everywhere.
