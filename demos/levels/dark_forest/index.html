Things to try

  1. The level loops. Red spawns at z = 0 and blue halfway round, at z = 80. Leave the red base north into the Glade side or south into the Bog side: both reach blue. The road through each base goes all the way round in x. View 3 (overview) and the #view=loop view show the copies of the level that light brings back round.
  2. Black holes hang over the two open areas, the Glade and the Bog. They bend sight lines across them, show a black shadow with a glowing disk and an Einstein ring, and you can't get closer than about 2 m. View 5.
  3. Wormholes are the fast routes. Each team has a Glade–Bog gate near its side of the middle: it switches you between the two ways to the other side. The Trench gate jumps from the west trench, near red, to the east trench, near blue (and back). Look into a mouth to see the far side; walk into its middle and you follow the same bent path as light. View 6 and 7.
  4. Shoot the laser (R) into a gate or past a black hole: it bends and goes through, and you see it lensed.
  5. Capture. Each pylon has 4 consoles. Go to the other team's pylon (#view=blue_pylon), face it and hold E for 10 s per console.
  6. Fly (H) above the canopy to see the zones: red moss and red lights in the red zone, blue in the blue zone, neutral amber lamps in the middle.
