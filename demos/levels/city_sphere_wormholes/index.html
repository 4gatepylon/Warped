Things to try

  1. Look up. You stand on the inside of a hollow sphere and gravity pulls you outward, so the floor curves up ahead of you and the far side of the city hangs overhead, around the glowing core. The glass ceiling 7 m up is the second floor.
  2. Climb to the glass. Four stairwells (8) lead up, toward the centre, through openings in the glass. Up there you stand on the inside of the glass sphere and look down through it at the streets. Both floors see each other, but the laser stops at the glass: fire up from the street and the beam ends on it.
  3. Wormholes. Each mouth sits in a dark doorway with a cyan frame (5, 6). From outside it bends light like a black hole; its disk shows the world around the other mouth, wrapped into rings (the far gate's cyan frame among them). Walk through the doorway to go through.
  4. Crosslink (equator, 5): joins the two street routes. Walk east through it and you come out of the other route's gate, still walking east.
  5. Lifts (6): from your own half of a street route straight onto the glass above the enemy zone. Fast, but defenders below see you through the glass, and you still have to come down a stairwell.
  6. Through a mouth you glide along a light ray (no gravity) until you are clear of it, then gravity takes over and your view rolls back upright in about a third of a second. Shoot the laser into a mouth and watch it come out of the other one.
  7. Win. Each pylon stands at its team's pole with 4 consoles. Press 9 (you play red next to the blue pylon) and hold E facing it: 10 s per console.
Zones: the blue and red zones are the caps around each pole (blue floor and glass, red floor and glass); the middle zone is the wide band between them. The middle has two street routes (A and B, on opposite sides of the sphere) and the glass skyway above everything. The other two sides of the band are sealed habitat blocks.
