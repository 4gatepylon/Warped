Things to try

  1. The black hole over the crater (5, or walk in from 4). Light bends around it: the crater rim behind it curves into an arc, a thin Einstein ring wraps the black shadow, and sight lines across the crater bend upward. Shoot the laser past it: the beam bends exactly as the light you see does, so you hit what the crosshair covers.
  2. The wormhole on the ridge route (6). Its disc shows the red landing pad, 116 m away. Walk into it (you move along light paths near a mouth, so aim at the disc) and you come out on that pad, 30 m from the red pylon, in full view of the red watchtower (7 looks back at the exit). Fast, but exposed. Shoot into it and the beam comes out there too.
  3. Far more room than it looks. From the overview (3) the whole infinite plain shrinks to a disc: the crater fills the middle and both spawns, 227 m apart, are squeezed against its rim. Distances along the flanks stretch by cosh(v/R): the ridge, 57 m off the centre line, is 211 m long though it spans only 96 m of the centre line, and a flank route that bows out 36 m is 36% longer than the 90 m centre route (28% in flat space).
  4. Long sight lines. Climb the ridge (9) and look across the crater. Things shrink faster with distance than in flat space (by R·sinh(d/R)/d: 2.4 times at 100 m), so a player 100 m away looks like one 240 m away would on Earth. Aim low: a level shot is a geodesic and the ground curves away beneath it, so 100 m out it passes 10 m up.
  5. Harsh light, long shadows. The sun hangs 17° above the horizon at the same height everywhere on the level, so every building throws a shadow three times its height. The planet low over the red base lights the shadows faintly.
  6. Take the red pylons (8 shows one of the three): hold E near a pylon's console, facing the pylon, for 10 s; letting go resets it. Red loses when all three are off. T switches team to attack the blue pylons.
  7. Fly (H) and climb: the horizon sinks below eye level, and the infinite ground closes into a shrinking disc under you.
