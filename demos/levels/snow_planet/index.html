Things to try

  1. The level loops. Both horizontal axes wrap around: walk 88 m from your spawn along the lengthwise axis, either way, and you reach red's spawn; keep going and you are home. Light wraps too, so further copies of the level fade into the haze (viewpoints 9 and 3).
  2. Two ways across. From blue's spawn, +x is the outpost way (huts, open snowfield, rock ridge) and −x the lake way (ice canyon, frozen lake, pine forest). Both reach red. Your own zone is open widthwise, so you can rotate between routes there.
  3. Wormholes join the two ways mid-field. W1 links the outpost courtyard to the ice canyon, W2 the top of the rock ridge to the pine forest. A mouth shows the far side inside a ring of bent images (viewpoint 6). Walk into its middle: near a mouth you move along light paths, so you are drawn through and come out still heading toward the enemy. The packed-snow path from your zone runs straight through the first hut into W1.
  4. Black holes hang over the open snowfield and the frozen lake (viewpoints 4, 5 and 7). They bend the sight lines across the open areas: look for the black shadow, the thin Einstein ring and the bent skyline.
  5. Ice is translucent. You see through the ice walls in the canyon and the windows of the huts and bases, but the laser stops on them.
  6. Fire the laser (R) into a mouth or past a black hole: the beam follows the true light path, and you see it lensed too.
  7. Win the level. Viewpoint 8 puts you at red's pylon. Hold E facing it for 10 s per console; letting go resets that console. T plays as red.
