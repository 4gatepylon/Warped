Things to try

  1. The space is hyperbolic (curvature radius 50 m): a circle of radius 100 m around you is 1.1 km long, not 630 m. Far things shrink much faster than at home, so the 20 m cliffs at the far end look tiny. Press 3: from 52 m up, the whole infinite desert fits in a disk below you.
  2. Three routes cross the middle: the open Salt Flats in the centre (long sight lines), the dry riverbed (on blue's right) and the sunken ruins (on blue's left). From blue spawn (1) the middle of each side route looks only 9° off the centre line (22° in flat space), but walking it is 35–40% longer than going straight across.
  3. The black hole hangs 16 m over the flats (4, 5) and bends every sight line across the middle: the far end of the flats is wrapped into a ring around its shadow. Fire the laser (R) just under it and fly round (H) to watch the beam swing round the shadow before it lands.
  4. The wormhole (6): the disk between the two obelisks shows blue's base, 111 m away. Walk into its middle: you come out 25 m from blue's pylon. It is red's fastest way in, but blue can watch the exit from the courtyard walls. Shoot through it too.
  5. Attack blue: press T to play red, take the wormhole, climb the dais and hold E facing blue's crystal. Five consoles, 10 s each; letting go resets the one you are on.
  6. Defend: as blue, take the corner stairs up to the courtyard's wall walk (9 is just outside it). The ziggurat in the ruins and the riverbed's arches are the other high points.
Choices: blocks are cubes in each structure's own Klein coordinates (exact geodesic faces; up to ~20% bigger at a big structure's edge). Warp objects bend light by a refractive index of the distance to their centre, faded out by 34 m (black hole) and 16 m (mouths). The black hole doesn't pull players; walking near a mouth bends your path like light, so you can walk in.
