Things to try

  1. Look up (view 1). The glass roof, the glowing core at the centre and, through two layers of glass, the far side of the city hanging upside down overhead.
  2. Walk down a street. The ground curves up in front of you; a level sight line meets the floor about 11 m ahead. The glass roof stops every shot along the outer floor within about 40 m.
  3. Climb to the glass floor (view 7): the stairs go up through a hole in the glass. On the glass you stand on the inside of a smaller sphere and look down at the streets.
  4. Shoot through the glass (view 4, the plaza): a red figure stands on the glass above you. You can see it, but the beam stops at the glass.
  5. Deactivate red's pylon (view 8): hold E facing it for 10 s per console, four times.
  6. The overview (view 3) cuts the sphere open. Blue's zone is the cap round the bottom pole, red's the cap round the top, and the middle zone is the wide band between them: the plaza, the alleys, and the street along the equator that joins them.
Blocks sit on a cube-sphere grid: each block is a piece of a spherical shell, and the layers get thinner toward the centre so blocks stay cube-shaped. The red half is blue's half turned 180° about an axis through the equator, so both teams play the same map.
