Warped

Warped needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 13 or later.

1. Drag Warped.app into your Applications folder.
2. Double-click it. The first time, macOS says it can't check Warped for malware, because the app
   isn't signed by Apple. Click Done (not Move to Trash), then do one of these, once:
   - Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway next to Warped,
     and confirm with your password or Touch ID. Then double-click Warped again and choose Open.
   - On macOS 13 or 14 only: right-click (or Control-click) Warped.app, choose Open, then Open
     again. (macOS 15 and later no longer offer this.)
   - In Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Warped.app
3. In the main menu, use the arrow keys and Return (Escape goes back):
   - Join a game found on your Wi-Fi, or host one; play alone or with bots; pick the five-level
     match or any one level.
   - Allow Local Network access when macOS asks, so games on your Wi-Fi can be found.

The logs of games you host, and the frame times of each level you play
(play_frame_times/<date>-<time>/frame_times.md), are in ~/Library/Logs/Warped/.
