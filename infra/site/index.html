Warped

A multiplayer shooter in curved space, with wormholes and black holes.

Install

Open Terminal (in Applications, Utilities), paste this line and press Return:

curl -fsSL @SITE_URL@/install.sh | bash

It puts Warped in Applications and opens it. Or download the zip and follow its note: the first time, macOS asks you to allow the app in System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway.

Play

  1. On the title screen, pick a game with Up and Down and press Return.
  2. For an online game, type the game's password once: ask whoever invited you.
  3. Click to capture the mouse. W A S D walk, Space jumps, the mouse button shoots, Tab opens the menu, Esc frees the mouse (twice quits).

Needs

  • A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 13 or later.
